# Provenance — Futures Triangle

Every element of `SKILL.md` that is attributed to a source, with where to find
it. Open the PDF at the printed page and search for the anchor phrase.
`python3 scripts/check_provenance.py --skills futures-triangle` checks every row.

The triangle is described in Inayatullah (2008) as a tool of the first pillar,
*Mapping*. His 2023 article on its origins (*World Futures Review*) is cited in
SOURCES.md but not downloaded, so no claim here rests on it.
