it. `python3 scripts/check_provenance.py --skills backcasting` checks every row.

numbers, so **`p. N` means PDF page N**. Inayatullah (2008) is cited by printed
page and is used for Elise Boulding's origin of the technique, memories of the
future, avoid mode, and plan versus action learning.

## fdbx adaptations (not from the sources)

- Pointing the method at design and product decisions.
- Describing the end state as "what people can do", "conditions that are true" and "what is no longer true".
- Refusing to invent the user's present-day figures, and the Unknowns to Fill section.
- The Backward Timeline table layout, the owner column, and "decision points" as dates by which a critical event must have happened. (The toolkit's decision points come from blending the forward and backward timelines, row 36.)
- The forward check as an optional step for a single analyst rather than two workshop groups.
