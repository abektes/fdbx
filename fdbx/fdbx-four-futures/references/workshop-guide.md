# Running the four futures with a group

Based on Dator (2009, pp. 7–12; provenance rows 11–16 and 27–32, 45–46),
summarised in our words. The AI-generated handouts replace Dator's written
future descriptions. They do not replace the group experience.

## Before

- **People.** At least 12, so each future has at least three. There is no upper limit: split into tables of at most ten, several tables per future.
- **Handouts.** One future per handout. Each participant sees only their own future until the debrief.
- **Setting.** Ideally four spaces, each dressed as its future with artifacts, pictures and sounds. If that is impossible, printed descriptions are enough.
- **Roles.** Each table chooses a reporter who keeps time and records answers. Dator's experience is that groups work better *without* facilitators, who tend to take over.

## During

Each handout opens with the welcome instructions (paraphrased): you have been placed in this future and cannot leave it; suspend disbelief; do not argue about whether it will happen or whether you like it; make the best of it, as you do of the present.

Then questions A–E (life in this future; probability; preferability; five things toward; five things against).

Ask everyone to write their own answers as well as the reporter. Dator notes that reporters' notes lose important detail.

## Debrief

1. Before each future's reporter speaks, read that future aloud to the whole room. This is the first time the others hear it.
2. Reporters present their group's answers.
3. Discuss the process. Point out, if it is true, that every group found a way to live well in its future: there is no best case or worst case.
4. Remind everyone that this was one step. The next is envisioning a preferred future, and say when that will happen.

Collect all written answers for analysis.

> **fdbx adaptation:** for a design team, add a fifth debrief question: "What did
> our product or service have to become to belong in your future?" Collect the
> answers as input to the skill's *robust design decisions* step.
