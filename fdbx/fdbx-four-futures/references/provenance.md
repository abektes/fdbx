# Provenance — Four Futures

Every element of `SKILL.md` that is attributed to a source, with where to find
it. Open the PDF at the printed page and search for the anchor phrase.
`python3 scripts/check_provenance.py --skills four-futures` checks every row.

Dator (2009) is the primary source. Inayatullah (2008) is used only for the
alias "Steady state" and the spiritual route to transformation, and both are
labelled as his in the skill.
