# Provenance — Horizon Scanning

Every element of `SKILL.md` that is attributed to a source, with where to find
it. `python3 scripts/check_provenance.py --skills horizon-scanning` checks every row.

The GO-Science *Futures Toolkit* is the only source. It has no printed page
numbers, so **`p. N` means PDF page N**. Its pages are laid out in two columns
and the extracted text interleaves them, so anchors are kept within one column.

## fdbx adaptations (not from the sources)

- Taking signals only from the user or a live search, and the "Leads to Verify" list for anything from model memory.
- The three modes (analyse, scan, plan).
- The rule against adding figures or details to user-supplied signals, and against describing a sourcing process that did not happen.
- The Scans table layout and the evidence-type column.
- Rating newness per theme alongside impact and likelihood in one table.
